# What your agents see on their phone

Your sales agents do almost all their work from a phone. The SalesDoctor mobile app is built for one job: visiting clients, taking orders, and reporting back to the office — even with a poor or no internet connection.

## Getting the app

Agents download **SalesDoctor** from the Play Store or App Store. After installing, they sign in with the same login and password you created in [Add agents](../getting-started/add-agents).

## What the agent's day looks like

| Step | What the agent does on the phone |
|------|----------------------------------|
| **1. Open the route** | Tap the first client on today's list |
| **2. Arrive at the shop** | The app captures GPS automatically; visit starts |
| **3. Take the order** | Pick products, set quantities — prices auto-fill |
| **4. (Optional) Audit** | Take photos of shelves, answer poll questions |
| **5. Close the visit** | Tap **Готово**. If something prevents the visit, pick a reason |

The order syncs to the office immediately if there's signal — or queues and syncs the next time the phone is back online.

## What you see in the office while they work

From your dashboard you see in real time:

![Dashboard with live team progress](/screens/guide/02-dashboard-home.webp)

- Where each agent is on the map
- Which clients they've visited
- Which clients they've skipped (with reason)
- Every order placed today

## Working offline

The app keeps a copy of these on the phone itself:

- The agent's route
- Every client's details and price list
- Every product with image and stock
- Every order taken today

So even with no signal, the agent can keep taking orders. Everything syncs automatically when they're back in coverage.

## Step 1 — Check sync status (in the office)

You don't need any special screen to confirm sync — when an agent's order lands, it appears in `/orders/list` instantly. If you don't see expected orders, the agent's phone is probably out of coverage.

## Step 2 — Watch KPI progress

Open `/dashboard/kpi` to see each agent's monthly progress:

![Monthly KPI dashboard](/screens/guide/22-dashboard-kpi.webp)

The progress bars match what the agent sees on their phone — they know in real time how far they are from each target.

## Tips

- **One login per device** — the app keeps the agent signed in for weeks.
- **Charge the phone overnight** — GPS + mobile data drain battery faster than messaging apps.
- **Update the app** when prompted — important new features arrive in monthly updates.
