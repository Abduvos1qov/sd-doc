# Visits and audits

A **visit** is more than just an order. While the agent is in the shop, they can also photograph the shelves, answer poll questions, and confirm prices. All this together is the **audit** — and it's a goldmine for managing your brand presence at every outlet.

## What the agent does on the phone

| Step | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| **1. Arrive at the shop** | The mobile app captures GPS automatically and marks the visit started |
| **2. Take the order** | Pick products, set quantities, prices auto-fill |
| **3. Photograph the shelves** | The app shows guidelines (centered, in focus) |
| **4. Photograph specific spots** | Freezer, cashier, entrance — whatever you configured |
| **5. Answer poll questions** | Yes/no, multiple choice, or short text |
| **6. Confirm prices** the shop is charging | Quick price audit |
| **7. Tap Done** | Visit closed, data syncs to the office |

The whole flow takes 2–3 minutes per shop.

## Step 1 — Open the photo report (in the office)

From the menu choose **Аудит → Фотоотчет** (Audit → Photo report), or open `/audit/photoReport` directly:

![Photo report — every photo taken by every agent today](/screens/guide/15-audit-photoreport.png)

## Step 2 — Apply filters

The top filter strip:

- **Дата** (Date) — single day or a range
- **Агент** (Agent) — one or many
- **Клиент** (Client) — drill into a specific outlet
- **Тип фото** (Photo type) — freezer / shelf / entrance / etc.

## Step 3 — Review one photo

Click a photo to enlarge. From the detail view you can:

- **Approve** — photo is good, agent gets credit toward KPI
- **Reject** — photo is blurry or wrong angle; agent gets feedback in the app and the photo doesn't count
- **Add a comment** — explanation for the agent

## Step 4 — Spot trends

Use the date-range slider to see how shelf presentation is changing over time. Common watch items:

- Competitor activity (new posters, new freezers)
- Stock-out signals (empty shelf where your product should be)
- Pricing slips (the shop is charging too low or too high)

## Why audits matter

| Without an audit | With an audit |
|------------------|---------------|
| You only see how much you sold | You see *why* you sold (or didn't) |
| Marketing changes go unnoticed | A new poster shows up in the next photo report |
| Competitors quietly take shelf space | You spot it the same week |
| Disputes happen over "I never got that" | A photo settles it in two seconds |

## Tips

- **Start small** — three or four photo types is enough. Don't ask for 20 photos per visit; quality drops.
- **Define what "good" looks like** — share one excellent photo with the team and they'll match it.
- **Use rejection feedback consistently** — after a week, photo quality across the team noticeably improves.
