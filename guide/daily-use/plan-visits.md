# Plan agent visits

A **visit plan** tells each agent which clients to visit on which day of the week. With a plan in place, every morning your team opens the mobile app and sees their route ready to go.

## Step 1 — Open the monthly planner

From the menu choose **Планы → Месяц** (Plans → Monthly), or open `/planning/monthly` directly:

![Monthly visit planner — calendar grid](/screens/guide/11-planning-monthly.png)

The grid shows every working day of the month, with planned visit counts per agent.

## Step 2 — Pick an agent (or a day)

Two ways to start planning:

- **Per agent** — click an agent's row to plan their full week at once
- **Per day** — click a day-column to plan that day across the team

## Step 3 — Add clients to a day

In the cell for an agent×day, click **+** (or just click on the cell). A picker appears with:

- **Региону / Территории** (Region / Territory) — narrow the list
- **Канал** (Channel) — filter by outlet type
- **Категория** (Category) — A/B/C client tiering

Tick the clients to add and click **Добавить**.

## Step 4 — Set frequency

For each client you can pick:

- **Каждую неделю** (Every week)
- **Раз в две недели** (Bi-weekly)
- **Раз в месяц** (Monthly)

The plan repeats automatically — set it once and it applies every week.

## Step 5 — Save

Click **Сохранить** at the top. The plan takes effect immediately:

- Agents see today's clients in their mobile app
- Supervisors see live progress in the dashboard

## What your agent sees on the phone

Each morning the agent's home screen shows today's planned clients with:

- The order they should visit them in (drag to reorder)
- A green tick once each is reached
- A red flag for skipped visits (with reason: shop closed, refused, no contact)

## Adjusting plans on the fly

Plans aren't permanent. You can:

- **Move a visit to another day** — drag the client to a new day cell
- **Skip a week** — pick "skip" on the client's row
- **Add a one-off visit** without changing the regular plan — the agent gets a notification

## Tips

- **Realistic numbers**: 20–30 clients per agent per day is healthy for most channels.
- **Cluster by geography**, not alphabet — zigzagging across town wastes hours.
- **Review monthly** with each supervisor. Drop clients that consistently refuse; add new ones the agent has opened.
