# Welcome to SalesDoctor 👋

You're about to set up SalesDoctor for your business. This short tour walks you through everything you need on day one — adding your team, your clients, your stock, and capturing your first order — with screenshots at every step.

![SalesDoctor — your dashboard at first sign-in](/screens/guide/02-dashboard-home.webp)

## What SalesDoctor does for you

SalesDoctor is a distribution platform for field sales teams. In one place you can:

- **Manage your sales team** — agents, supervisors and expeditors with their own roles and permissions.
- **Keep a single customer list** — every shop, restaurant or outlet you sell to, with full visit and order history.
- **Track stock and warehouses** — see what's on hand at any moment, across one or many locations.
- **Capture orders** — from the office, from the road on a phone, or via your own online catalogue.
- **See the numbers** — sales, debts, visits and KPI for every agent, every day.

## A note on screenshots

Every page in this guide includes screenshots of the actual SalesDoctor interface. If a screen on your account looks slightly different, it usually means a feature is turned on or off for you — your administrator can adjust this in **Settings**.
