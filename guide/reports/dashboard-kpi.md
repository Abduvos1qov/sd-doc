# Dashboard and KPI

The dashboard is your business pulse — every important number for today, in one place. After a few weeks of real data, the dashboard becomes your single best view of how the business is doing. **Each tile is clickable** and drills into a detailed view.

## Step 1 — Open the home dashboard

From the menu click the **logo** (top left), or open `/dashboard/supervayzer` directly:

![Supervisor home dashboard with KPI tiles + agent row](/screens/guide/39-supervisor-dashboard-full.webp)

The page splits into three regions:

1. **Top headline tiles** — today's most important numbers
2. **Middle charts** — trends across the period (visits, sales, returns)
3. **Bottom agent table** — per-agent progress for the day

## Step 2 — Drill into "Продажи" (Sales)

Click the **Sales** tile (or open `/dashboard/sales` directly). A sales-focused dashboard opens:

![Sales drill-down — by agent, by region, by product group](/screens/guide/34-dashboard-sales.webp)

You see:
- Sales totals split by **agent**, **region**, **product group**
- A **chart strip** comparing today vs the same day last week
- **Top clients** by spend
- **Returns** as a separate bar — knowing what came back is as important as knowing what went out

## Step 3 — Drill into "Финансы" (Debt / Finance)

Click the **Debt** tile (or open `/dashboard/finans` directly):

![Finance drill-down — debts, payments, cashbox totals](/screens/guide/35-dashboard-finans.webp)

This page consolidates:
- **Total receivable** — what your clients still owe you, broken down by age
- **Today's payments** — by cashbox, by collection method
- **Aged debt buckets** — under 7 days, 7–30, 30+
- **Top debtors** by amount — your collections call list

Click any line item to open the client's full transaction history.

## Step 4 — Drill into "Визиты" (Visit report)

Click the **Visits** tile, or open `/report/visit` directly:

![Visit report — coverage map per agent](/screens/guide/37-report-visit.webp)

The visit report answers two questions at once:
- **Coverage** — what fraction of planned clients each agent actually reached
- **Quality** — how long they stayed, what they captured (orders, photos), what they skipped (with reason)

## Step 5 — Open the monthly KPI dashboard

For the long view across the whole team, open `/dashboard/kpi`:

![Monthly KPI dashboard — agent-by-agent progress bars](/screens/guide/38-dashboard-kpi-full.webp)

This is the agent-by-agent monthly progress board. Each agent has their own row with progress bars per indicator.

## Step 6 — Read each KPI

Common indicators on the KPI dashboard:

| KPI | What it measures |
|-----|------------------|
| **Sales target** | Money in orders the agent should produce this month |
| **AKB target** | Active clients the agent should keep |
| **OKB target** | Clients the agent should visit at all |
| **Visit target** | Visits per day or per month |
| **MML target** | "Must-Match List" — products that must be present in every shop |
| **Photo target** | Audit photos per visit |

Two or three indicators is plenty — more dilutes attention.

## Step 7 — Set a target for an agent

1. Open the agent's profile (**Команда → Агенты** → click name)
2. Open the **KPI** tab
3. For each indicator, set the **target for this month**
4. Click **Сохранить**

The agent immediately sees their target on the mobile-app home screen with a progress bar that fills in as the month goes on.

## End-of-month bonus calculation

If you pay bonuses based on KPI:

1. On the last working day of the month, KPI numbers freeze
2. Each agent's bonus is calculated against the targets you set
3. The result appears in the KPI report — finance uses it for payroll

## Tips

- **Pick KPIs that the agent controls** — punishing low sales when stock keeps running out destroys morale.
- **Two or three KPIs is plenty** — more than five gets diluted.
- **Show KPI on the agent's phone home screen** — visibility alone drives behaviour change.
- **Click everything** — every number on the dashboard is a link into a detail report. If you wonder what made up a number, click it.
