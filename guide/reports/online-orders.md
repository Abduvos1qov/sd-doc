# Online orders

If your clients want to order **without waiting for an agent's visit**, you can give them a self-service catalogue — a website or Telegram bot where they pick products, place orders, and even pay online.

## Step 1 — Open the online orders inbox

From the menu choose **Заявки → Онлайн заявки** (Orders → Online orders), or open `/onlineOrder/order` directly:

![Online orders inbox](/screens/guide/16-online-orders.webp)

This list shows every order placed via the online portal or Telegram bot — distinct from orders captured by agents on the mobile app.

## Step 2 — Read the status column

Each online order can be in one of:

- **Новая** (New) — just placed by the client, awaiting confirmation
- **Подтверждена** (Confirmed) — accepted, ready for delivery
- **Оплачена** (Paid) — client paid online (if the gateway is enabled)
- **Доставлена** (Delivered) — completed
- **Отменена** (Cancelled) — withdrawn before delivery

## Step 3 — Confirm an order

Click a **Новая** order to open it. Review the lines, then:

1. Click **Подтвердить** (Confirm) to accept it as-is
2. Or edit quantities/prices first if you need to (e.g. an item is out of stock)
3. Or click **Отменить** (Cancel) if you can't fulfil it

Confirmed online orders flow into your normal orders list just like agent-captured orders.

## Step 4 — Assign delivery

From the confirmed order:

- Pick a **Склад** (warehouse)
- Pick an **Экспедитор** (expeditor)
- Pick a **Дата отгрузки** (delivery date)
- Save

The order now appears on the expeditor's mobile app for delivery.

## Step 5 — SMS the client

Once confirmed (and again on dispatch), the system sends an SMS to the client. You can configure the SMS text in **Настройки → SMS шаблоны** (Settings → SMS templates). Common templates:

- **Order received** — "Your order #123 is confirmed."
- **Out for delivery** — "Your order is on the way, ETA 30 minutes."
- **Delivered** — "Order #123 delivered. Total: 1,500,000 UZS."

## Turning the portal on for a client

1. Open the client's profile
2. Switch on **Онлайн заявки** (Online ordering)
3. Set their **онлайн логин** and **пароль** (or let the system generate)
4. Share the URL and credentials with the client

The client can sign in from any browser and start ordering.

## Tips

- **Start with your top 20 clients** — frequent buyers get the most value from the portal.
- **Don't force everyone online** — some clients prefer the agent visit. Both channels coexist.
- **Keep product photos sharp** — the catalogue is the first thing the client sees; a good photo doubles confidence.
