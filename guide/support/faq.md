# Frequently asked questions

The most common questions from new SalesDoctor users.

## Accounts and access

### Can two people share one login?

No. Each user — agent, supervisor, manager, cashier — should have their own login. Sharing logins makes visits, orders and KPI impossible to attribute correctly.

### How do I reset a password?

If you're an administrator, open **Team → [user list] → click the user → Reset password**. Otherwise ask your administrator.

### What happens when someone leaves the team?

Don't delete the account — that would erase their history. Instead open their profile and turn **Active** off. Their login stops working immediately, but every visit, order and number they produced stays in the system.

## Orders

### Can I edit an order after delivery?

Technically yes, but we strongly recommend against it. Edits to delivered orders confuse both the client and finance. If you must change something, add a **new** order (or a return) and leave the original in place.

### A client refused part of an order. What happens?

The expeditor records the refused items on the mobile app at the door. The system:

1. Removes those items from the invoice
2. Returns them to your defect warehouse
3. Adjusts the client's bill

You see the change on the dashboard within seconds.

### Can I cancel a delivered order?

The cleanest way is a **full return**: open the order, tap **Return all**. The goods come back, the bill cancels itself, the client's balance returns to what it was.

## Stock and warehouses

### Where do I see what's in stock right now?

**Warehouse → Stock balance**, with filters for the specific warehouse you care about.

### A product shows in stock but my agent can't add it to an order. Why?

The most common reasons:

1. The product is in a **warehouse the agent doesn't have access to** (e.g. another branch).
2. The product is in a **defect warehouse** — it's there but not for resale.
3. The product is **inactive** — flag turned off in the catalogue.

### How do I add a new product?

**Settings → Products → + New**. Your administrator usually does this.

## Payments

### How do I record cash from an expeditor?

The expeditor records it on their phone at the door, then the cashier approves the cash at the end of the day. See [Sales & debts](../reports/sales-and-debts) for the full flow.

### A client says they paid but the system shows debt. What now?

1. Ask which day they paid and how much.
2. Open **Finans → Payments** and search by client + that date.
3. If the payment is there but not approved, the cashier approves it and the debt clears.
4. If it isn't there, the payment was never recorded — record it manually now.

## Mobile

### The agent's phone won't sync. What do I do?

Most sync problems clear up with two steps:

1. Confirm the phone is online (open a browser, load a website).
2. In the SalesDoctor app, pull down on the home screen to force a refresh.

If orders are still stuck, sign out and back in. If that doesn't help, contact support.

### Can agents work offline?

Yes — the app keeps everything on the phone and syncs when there's signal. See [Agent app](../mobile/agent-app) for details.
