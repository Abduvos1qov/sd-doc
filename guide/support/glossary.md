# Glossary

A short list of words you'll meet often.

| Term | What it means |
|------|---------------|
| **Agent** | A salesperson who visits clients, takes orders, sometimes delivers them. |
| **AKB** | "Active Client Base" — clients who placed at least one order in a given period (usually a month). |
| **Audit** | A check on a shop — photos of shelves, prices, posters, sometimes poll questions. |
| **Branch / Filial** | A regional office or subdivision of your business with its own staff and warehouse. |
| **Cashier** | A person who counts and approves cash collected by expeditors at the end of the day. |
| **Channel** | The type of outlet — small shop, supermarket, restaurant, kiosk. Often determines pricing and promotions. |
| **Client / Outlet** | Any place you sell to: a shop, restaurant, kiosk. Used interchangeably. |
| **Credit limit** | The maximum a client can owe you at any one time. |
| **Defect** | Goods that came back from a client and can't be resold (damaged, expired). |
| **Discount** | A reduction in price, either fixed or as a percentage. |
| **Expeditor** | The person who drives the van and delivers orders. |
| **KPI** | "Key Performance Indicator" — a target you measure each agent or supervisor against. |
| **Manager** | A user who oversees several supervisors and the whole sales operation. |
| **MML** | "Must-Match List" — a set of products that should be present in every shop you serve. |
| **OKB** | "Overall Client Base" — every client your team has visited or sold to. Always bigger than AKB. |
| **Order** | A request from a client for specific goods at agreed prices for delivery on a chosen date. |
| **Outlet** | Another word for client — a shop, kiosk, restaurant. |
| **Photo report** | A daily collection of photos taken by agents during audits. |
| **Plan** | Either a visit plan (which clients to visit which day) or a sales plan (how much to sell). |
| **Price type** | A named price list — "Retail", "Wholesale", "Dealer". Each client buys at one specific price type. |
| **Recovery** | Restoring a cancelled order. |
| **RFM** | A way of classifying clients by **R**ecency (when they last bought), **F**requency (how often) and **M**onetary value (how much). |
| **Return** | Goods sent back by a client — either partial (some items) or full (the whole order). |
| **Route** | A daily list of clients an agent will visit. |
| **Supervisor** | A user who oversees a small team of agents. |
| **Tara** | Empty crates, bottles, packaging that needs to come back from the client. |
| **Territory / Region** | Geographic area used to slice reports — district, city zone, region. |
| **Trip** | An expeditor's day of deliveries — a planned load, a list of stops, a return. |
| **Van stock** | Goods currently on an expeditor's van. Treated as a separate warehouse. |
| **Visit** | An agent's stop at one client. Can include an order, an audit, both, or neither. |
| **Warehouse** | A physical (or logical) place stock lives. |
