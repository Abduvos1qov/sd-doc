## Asking your administrator

Most day-to-day questions are answered fastest by your in-house **administrator** — the person who set up SalesDoctor for your business. They can:

- Reset passwords
- Add or remove team members
- Adjust permissions
- Turn features on or off
- Pull custom reports

If you don't know who your administrator is, ask the person who gave you your login.

## Contacting SalesDoctor support

For issues your administrator can't solve — for example a feature that isn't working as described in this guide — reach out to support:

- **In the app** — open your account menu (top right) and pick **Help**. You can send a message that includes which screen you were on at the time.
- **By phone** — your administrator has the support hotline number.
- **By email** — contact details are on your contract.

When contacting support, tell them:

1. **Which screen** you were on (e.g. "the order list, filtered by today")
2. **What you tried to do** (e.g. "edit an order I'd just delivered")
3. **What happened** (e.g. "got a red error saying 'permission denied'")
4. **A screenshot** if you can — it saves a lot of back-and-forth
