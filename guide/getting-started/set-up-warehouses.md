# Set up your warehouses

A **warehouse** is any place you keep stock — your main depot, a satellite store, an expeditor's van. Every product unit you sell exists in exactly one warehouse at any time.

## Step 1 — Open the warehouses list

From the menu choose **Склад → Склады** (Warehouse → Warehouses), or open `/warehouse/list` directly:

![Warehouses list — all branches and types](/screens/guide/08-warehouses-list.png)

The list shows every warehouse with its type, person responsible, and current balance value.

## Step 2 — Decide what types you need

Most distribution businesses end up with these:

| Type | Example name | Purpose |
|------|--------------|---------|
| **Main** | "Central depot" | Receives from suppliers |
| **Filial** | "Tashkent branch" | Stock at a regional office |
| **Van stock** | "Van #07" | What an expeditor is carrying |
| **Defect** | "Returns / damaged" | Goods that came back and can't be resold |

## Step 3 — Click "Add warehouse"

Click **+ Добавить** at the top right.

## Step 4 — Fill in the form

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| **Название** | Name your team will recognise |
| **Тип** | Main / Filial / Van / Defect |
| **Ответственный** | The team member who can sign for receipts and transfers |
| **Адрес** | Physical address (or "Van #07" for vehicles) |

## Step 5 — Save

Click **Сохранить**. The warehouse appears in the list immediately. You can now move stock into it via **Поступления** (Purchases) — covered next.

## What you can do from each warehouse

Click any warehouse row to open its card:

- **Current balance** — every product, how many of each, value
- **Movements** — every receipt in, sale out, transfer to another warehouse
- **Inventory checks** — what's actually on the shelves vs what the system says

## Tips

- **One main warehouse is enough** to start with — you can add more later.
- **A separate "defect" warehouse** makes month-end balancing much easier.
- **Don't change a warehouse type** after you've started using it — create a new one instead.
