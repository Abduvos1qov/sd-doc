# Your dashboard at a glance

The dashboard is the page that opens after you sign in. It's the most-used page in SalesDoctor — every important number for today lives here.

## Step 1 — Open the dashboard

Click the **SalesDoctor logo** (top left) from any page to come back here, or just open `/dashboard/supervayzer` directly.

![Full dashboard view](/screens/guide/02-dashboard-home.png)

## Step 2 — Read the top bar

Along the very top you'll see:

- **Logo / Home** (top left) — back to this page
- **Notification bell** — new orders, finished deliveries, approval requests
- **Your account menu** (top right) — switch languages, change password, sign out

## Step 3 — Read the left menu

The vertical menu on the left lists every workspace you can open. The common entries for a manager or administrator:

- **Планы** (Plans) — visit and sales planning
- **Заявки** (Orders) — sales and returns
- **Склад** (Warehouse) — stock, purchases, transfers
- **Маркировка** (Markirovka) — CIS / EDI compliance
- **Клиенты** (Clients) — your customer list
- **Команда** (Team) — auditors, agents, supervisors, expeditors
- **Аудит 2** (Audit 2) — merchandising audit
- **Настройки** (Settings) — prices, currencies, params

If a menu entry is missing for you, it usually means your role doesn't need it.

## Step 4 — Read the KPI tiles in the middle

Each tile in the main area is a today-or-this-period number:

- **Sales today** — total value of all orders placed today
- **Visits** — completed agent visits
- **AKB** — Active Client Base (clients who ordered this month)
- **OKB** — Overall Client Base (every client your team reaches)
- **Debt** — total amount your clients still owe you
- **Orders waiting** — new orders not yet shipped

Click any tile to drill into the detail behind that number.

## Step 5 — Change the date range

A date-range picker at the top of the dashboard lets you compare today with yesterday, last week, or last month. Use it to spot trends.

## Tips

- **Bookmark this page** in your browser for one-click access.
- **Click an agent's name** in the supervisor view to see their day in detail.
- **Open in a new tab** (`Ctrl+Click` or `Cmd+Click`) so the dashboard stays in one tab while you work in another.
