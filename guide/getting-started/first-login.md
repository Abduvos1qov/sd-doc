# Your first login

This page walks you through signing in to SalesDoctor for the very first time. Each step shows what you'll see on screen.

## Step 1 — Open your workspace

Your administrator gives you a link that looks like `https://yourcompany.salesdoc.io` (or `http://localhost:8080` if running on your own machine). Open it in **Google Chrome** (recommended) or any modern browser.

You'll land on the sign-in form:

![Login screen — login, password, "Remember me", "Sign in"](/screens/guide/01-login-screen.webp)

## Step 2 — Enter your login and password

- **Логин** — the user name your administrator created for you (e.g. `admin`, `manager1`, `agent.askar`)
- **Пароль** — the password they gave you
- **Запомнить меня** — tick this if you trust the device; you'll stay signed in for two weeks

Click **Войти** (Sign in).

:::tip First-time password
If this is your very first sign-in, ask your administrator to set you up with a unique password — don't keep the default one for long.
:::

## Step 3 — See your home dashboard

After a successful sign-in you land on your **home dashboard**. What you see depends on your role:

![Home dashboard after sign-in](/screens/guide/02-dashboard-home.webp)

- **Administrators / managers** — wide overview of all today's numbers
- **Supervisors** — their team's progress for today
- **Cashiers** — open straight into the payment approval queue

If you'd rather start with how to read the dashboard, jump to **[Dashboard tour →](./dashboard-tour)**.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| "Wrong login or password" | The password is case-sensitive. If still no luck, ask your administrator to reset it. |
| Page won't load | Try a different browser, or check your internet connection. |
| "License expired" screen | Contact your administrator — your subscription may need renewal. |
